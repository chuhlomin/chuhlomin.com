# Meeting Alerter

I have been working on this app for a couple of years to help me navigate my crazy days full of meetings.

It lives in the MacOS menu bar, shows what is coming up, reminds me when a meeting starts, and opens Zoom in one click.

![meeting-alerter.png](meeting-alerter.png "Meeting Alerter Promo")

At first, I planned to launch it with Apple Calendar support only. But EventKit does not expose Google Calendar attachments, so I had to add direct Google Calendar support too.

With help from LLMs, I was finally able to get it into a shippable state.

https://meeting-alerter.app/
